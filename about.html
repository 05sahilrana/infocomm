Who we are

A research group built around one question: how far can wireless networks go?

We are faculty, postdoctoral fellows, PhD and M.Tech (Research) scholars in the School of Computing and Electrical Engineering, IIT Mandi. Our work ranges from the fundamental limits of information flow to algorithms for massive and cell-free MIMO, integrated sensing and communication, and battery-free IoT.

We value work that holds up both on paper and on hardware. Ideas are analysed rigorously, tested in simulation, and validated on software-defined radio testbeds.

What we work on

Research thrust areas

Massive & Cell-Free MIMO

Detection, channel estimation and pilot design for massive, cell-free and XL-MIMO networks.

Integrated Sensing & Communication

Networks that communicate and sense at once — localization with zero extra overhead.

Information Theory & Network Coding

Entropy regions, capacity bounds and the limits of information flow in networks.

Intelligent Reflecting Surfaces

Programmable surfaces that reshape the radio environment for coverage and efficiency.

Backscatter & Wireless Power

Ambient backscatter and wirelessly powered networks for battery-free IoT.

Wireless LANs & IoT Networks

Resource allocation, D2D and next-generation WLANs for dense, connected systems.

Experimental facilities

From equations to over-the-air

Our lab is equipped for real-world experimentation, so results can be reproduced and taken beyond simulation.

  • Software-defined radios

    USRP platforms for PHY-layer prototyping and over-the-air experiments.

  • Wi-Fi testbeds

    Open WLAN experimentation, including the POWER platform.

  • Multi-antenna platforms

    Setups for MIMO, distributed and cell-free experimentation.

  • Embedded & IoT systems

    Low-power nodes for sensing, backscatter and IoT deployments.
