Research workspace

InfoComm Lab

The shared home of our research group — a sitting and working area open to all students, where they carry out independent research, simulations and writing.

What’s available

  • Seating for all studentsDedicated desks for independent research
  • WorkstationsShared computers for simulation and writing
  • Campus networkWired and Wi-Fi connectivity
  • Discussion spaceGroup meetings and research discussions
  • Stationery & suppliesEveryday stationery and small equipment on request
  • Quiet studyA calm place to read, code and write papers

What you can do here

  • Work on your independent research every day
  • Run simulations and analyse results
  • Write papers and discuss ideas with labmates

Access: open to all lab students.

Signal processing & communication

SPComm Lab

A hands-on signal processing and communication lab for measuring, generating and analysing real radio signals — from bench measurements to SDR links.

Equipment

  • Digital storage oscilloscopes (DSO)Capture and analyse time-domain signals
  • USRP B210 SDRs2×2 MIMO, 70 MHz – 6 GHz, USB 3.0
  • Signal / function generatorsTest signals and waveforms
  • Spectrum analyserFrequency-domain measurements
  • Antennas & RF accessoriesAntennas, cables, attenuators, splitters
  • Workstations with GNU Radio / MATLABSDR programming and signal analysis

What you can do here

  • Build and test transmit/receive chains on USRP B210
  • Measure modulation, spectrum and channel effects
  • Course labs and student projects in DSP and communications

Access: managed by the faculty in-charge of the lab. Open to lab members most of the time, with permission from the assigned faculty.

5G & beyond

Nexus Lab

Our 5G lab: an end-to-end 5G NR setup for research and live demonstrations, with connected drones and cameras for real-world use cases.

Equipment

  • End-to-end 5G NR setupOpen-source gNB and 5G core (e.g. OpenAirInterface)
  • SDR radio unitsUSRP-based 5G radio front-ends
  • 5G user equipment5G modules and handsets as UEs
  • DroneConnected-drone experiments over 5G
  • CCTV camerasLive video streaming and surveillance demos
  • Edge computing serversLow-latency processing at the network edge

What you can do here

  • Deploy and test a private 5G network end to end
  • Demonstrate drone and live-video use cases over 5G
  • Experiment with cell-free / multi-RU and 6G ideas

Access: managed by the faculty in-charge of the lab. Open to lab members most of the time, with permission from the assigned faculty.

Want to use SPComm Lab or Nexus Lab for your project? Request permission from the lab’s faculty in-charge.
