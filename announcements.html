2026

  • Oct 8, 2026Admissions open

    Admissions open for M.Tech (Research), PhD and Dual Degree programs at SCEE, IIT Mandi (Jan–May 2027 session). The InfoComm Lab is part of the Intelligent Communication Systems (ICS) group of SCEE — applicants interested in wireless communications, cell-free massive MIMO, ISAC, signal processing, information theory and 5G/6G testbeds are encouraged to apply under ICS.

    Last date to apply: November 4, 2026, 5:00 PM.

    Apply: SCEE PG admissionsAdvertisement (PDF)Programs & pathways

  • Oct 7–10, 2026

    InfoComm Lab at India Mobile Congress (IMC) 2026. Researchers from the lab, together with Dr. Adarsh Patel, showcased their 5G and 6G work through research posters and live demonstrations at the IIT Mandi booth (Hall 1, Academic Area) at Yashobhoomi, New Delhi. The event was inaugurated by the Hon’ble Prime Minister.

    Team: N V Divya Lakshmi, Ishita Sharma, Sahil Rana, Anshuman Thakur, Abhay Upadhyay and Shivam Rajput. Topics showcased:

    • Testbed development for cell-free massive MIMO systems
    • ISAC for 6G: from connectivity to intelligent perception
    • Non-antipodal signaling based semi-blind detector in uplink cell-free mMIMO networks
    • AI-native O-RAN RRM: building xApps and rApps for 5G-Advanced/6G
    • Design and development of algorithms for RIS-assisted wireless systems
    • Fast detection algorithms for next-generation communication networks
